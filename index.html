Pokérole VTT

A virtual tabletop for the Pokérole tabletop RPG, built as two self-contained HTML files. No install, no server, no accounts. Open one and it runs.

⚔ Open the GM toolkit Combat tracker, the full dex, cast & NPCs, encounters, shops, campaign planner, tournaments. 📱 Open the player app Trainer sheet, team and bag — and your half of a battle, on your own screen.
Start with the GM toolkit. Go to ⚔ Combat → 🎯 Spawn, type any species, and add it to the field. Everything — 1,200 species, 860 moves, 300 abilities — is baked into the file you just loaded. Multiplayer needs a free Firebase project of your own; single-screen play needs nothing at all.

What's in it

  • Combat that knows the rules. Dice pools built off the sheet, the full reaction window, statuses with real durations, weather and terrain, and 300 abilities that fire on their own rather than sitting in a tooltip.
  • Players roll their own dice. When the GM attacks, the target's screen shows the incoming successes, their dodge pool, and what dodging costs them.
  • A campaign planner that runs sessions — scenes, skill checks, encounters and rewards as structured data, played beat by beat instead of read off a document.
  • The whole economy — shops, crafting, breeding, day care, training, trading, and a Pokédex that unlocks in tiers as you meet things.
  • Set pieces — tournaments with brackets and side betting, contests, a Safari Zone, a game corner, boss framing with lair actions.
